VibeComics

Build a comic with an AI agent, and actually make it good.

VibeComics keeps your agent using the same characters, scenes and style across every panel, so you refine the art instead of rerolling it. Not one-shot AI slop.

  1. Paste it into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or any AI agent that can run commands.
  2. It asks what your comic is about, and where to keep it: your Google Drive, or storage of your own.
  3. Watch it write, draw and refine the comic, page by page.
