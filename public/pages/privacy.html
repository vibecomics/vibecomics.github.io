Privacy Policy

VibeComics. Last updated: 20 September 2026.

Back to the app · Terms of Service · Source code on GitHub

Short version: we do not store anything. VibeComics has no server, no database, no user accounts and no advertising. Your comics live on your own Google Drive, and we never receive them. We do use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used; see below.

What VibeComics is

VibeComics is a static website. All of its code runs in your browser. The complete source code is public at https://github.com/nparashuram/vibecomic/, so you can check every statement on this page against the code.

Your comics

A comic is saved as a project file (project.json) and its image files, inside a folder on your Google Drive. The app reads and writes those files directly from your browser to Google. They do not pass through any server of ours, because we do not run one.

Google Drive access

VibeComics asks for one Google permission, the drive.file scope. It lets the app see and change only the files and folders that the app itself created. It cannot see the rest of your Drive.

The access token Google gives the app is kept only in the memory of the open page. It is not written to cookies or to any storage, and reloading the page discards it. Using “Disconnect” in the app revokes the grant at Google. You can also remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account settings, under third-party access.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs by VibeComics adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The information is used only to open, show, edit and save your comic. It is not transferred to anyone, used for advertising, or read by people.

What is stored in your browser

  • One interface preference (how tall the editor's bottom panel is), kept in your browser's local storage. It contains no personal data and no comic content.
  • A copy of the app's own files (its code and images), kept by the browser's cache so the site loads faster. It contains no comic content.
  • Cookies set by Google Analytics (see below), used to distinguish visitors and measure usage of the site.

Analytics

VibeComics uses Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, for example which pages are visited and how often. Google Analytics collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser type, and the pages you visit, and sets cookies to do so. It does not have access to your comics or your Google Drive content. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles this data.

Third parties

  • Google. Signing in and using Drive involves Google, which handles that data under Google's Privacy Policy. The page loads Google's sign-in script from Google's servers.
  • Google Analytics. The page loads Google's analytics script to measure site usage, as described above.
  • Hosting. The site is served as static files, for example from GitHub Pages. Like any web host, the host may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP addresses and requested pages) under its own policy. The app does not collect those logs and does not have access to them.

AI assistants

The editor exposes a scripting interface in the page so that an AI assistant you choose to run against your own browser session can operate it. VibeComics does not send your comic to any assistant. What you share with an assistant, and what it does with it, is between you and that assistant's provider.

Your control

Your comics are ordinary files in your own Drive. You can open, copy, move or delete them there whenever you like, and you can revoke the app's access whenever you like. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date. The full history is in the public repository.
